# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in Liftr welcoming and free from
harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of technical expertise.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information without
permission are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the Liftr project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Actions
will be proportionate to the circumstances and will prioritize the safety of
the community.

Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/sithea-nou). Do not open a
public issue containing sensitive personal details. Reports will be reviewed
as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
