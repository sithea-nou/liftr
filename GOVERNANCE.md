# Governance

Liftr is currently maintained by [Sithea Nou](https://github.com/sithea-nou).
The governance model is intentionally lightweight while the project is young.

## Decisions

Routine changes are decided through pull-request review. Contributors should
seek rough consensus and document important tradeoffs. The maintainer makes the
final decision when consensus cannot be reached.

Changes to public contracts, lifecycle semantics, security boundaries, or
major architecture require an Architecture Decision Record under `docs/adr`.
Accepted ADRs remain the authoritative record for those decisions.

## Contributions

Anyone may propose issues, documentation, tests, or code. Contributions are
reviewed for correctness, scope, maintainability, and consistency with the
project's architectural principles. Submission does not guarantee acceptance.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- reviewing and merging contributions;
- managing releases and repository settings;
- handling vulnerability and conduct reports;
- protecting compatibility and architectural boundaries; and
- appointing additional maintainers when sustained contribution and project
  needs justify it.

Material governance changes should be proposed publicly and recorded in this
file.
